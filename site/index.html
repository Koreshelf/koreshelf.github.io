A library all your own

A free program to keep your EPUB and PDF ebooks organized and to read them. Download one file and open it: there’s nothing to install and you don’t need an account.

Chapter I

The shelf

Add your EPUBs and PDFs and they’re arranged on a 3D shelf. The app looks up the cover, title and author. You can split your books across several shelves and find them by searching for a title or author.

Chapter II

Reading

You read right in the app, page by page or scrolling, with a light or dark theme. The app remembers where you left off. The sentences you underline end up on a board, book by book.

Chapter III

Your books stay on your device

You don’t need to create an account and there’s no subscription. Your books stay in a folder on your computer or phone and aren’t uploaded to the internet. The code is public on GitHub, under the MIT license.

iPhone and iPad, for advanced users

Apple only allows apps from outside the App Store through dedicated tools. For now, KoreShelf on iPhone and iPad takes a few extra steps.

  1. On a computer, install AltStore or Sideloadly and sign in with your Apple ID.
  2. Download the KoreShelf .ipa file.
  3. Connect your iPhone or iPad to the computer and install the file with the tool you chose.
  4. On the device, trust the developer in Settings › General › VPN & Device Management.
Download the .ipa file

With a free Apple ID the app must be refreshed every 7 days (AltStore does it for you). From iOS 16 you also need to turn on Developer Mode in Settings › Privacy & Security.
